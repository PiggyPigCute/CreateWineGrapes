
⚙️ Create: 🍷 Wine & Grapes 🍇
=======
**Create: Wine & Grapes** is an addon of the Minecraft mod Create adding all the *viniculture* process. Plant, press, crush, fermentate, distillate, debourb, drink (with moderation)! Become a specialist and discover the 32 famous wines in this mod each has its own grape variety, its own climate and environnemental conditions and its own processing steps.
